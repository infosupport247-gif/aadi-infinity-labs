StoryNest Privacy Policy

Draft — pending developer verification. This page is not a finalized privacy notice and should not be submitted to Apple until the actual app data practices and legal operator have been confirmed.

Scope

This draft concerns StoryNest, a storytelling application intended for children and families.

Information and permissions

Before publication of the final policy, the developer must identify information collected by the app and its SDKs, including any story inputs, account information, device identifiers, diagnostics, photos, audio and permissions, and explain the purposes and recipients of each category.

Children and parents

The final policy must explain applicable age restrictions, parental notice and consent, parental access and deletion rights, and safeguards for children's information. Parents may contact the support address below with privacy questions.

AI and third parties

Any AI providers, analytics, advertising, hosting or other third-party processing must be identified and their handling of personal information explained. Whether these services are used has not yet been verified.

Subscriptions

Apple provides subscription management through Apple Account settings. The final policy must explain what purchase or entitlement information StoryNest receives and retains.

Storage, retention and rights

The final policy must specify storage locations, retention periods, security measures, deletion procedures, applicable privacy rights and the legal identity of the responsible operator. Privacy requests may be directed to the support address below; do not include passwords or payment details.
